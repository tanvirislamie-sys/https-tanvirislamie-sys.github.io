Lean thinkingKaizen

Find the waste. Fix it. Lock it in. Repeat.

I learned process improvement through university coursework in lean manufacturing and work study, and I hold a Lean Six Sigma White Belt from GoLeanSixSigma. At Akij Cement I put it to work, following a five-packer plant from raw material intake to packing and mapping where the flow broke down.

The 3D line follows those plant stages. Each Kaizen cycle shrinks the work-in-progress piles and speeds up the flow. The PDCA wheel then rolls one step up the slope, with a standard wedged behind it so the gain can’t slip back. Figures are illustrative.
